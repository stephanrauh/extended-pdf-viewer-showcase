Starting with version 32.0.0, you can put several <ngx-extended-pdf-viewer> tags on the same page.

Each viewer shows its own document and keeps its own page, zoom, search, form fields, annotation editors and print job. Keyboard shortcuts and printing with window.print() or CTRL+P act on the viewer you've clicked or focused last.

Two things are shared by every viewer on the page:

  • pdfDefaultOptions apply to every viewer.
  • All viewers share one theme. A dark viewer next to a light viewer isn't supported.
